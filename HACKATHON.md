## Devpost submission form — exact fields

These are the literal fields on the draft submission wizard, read from the live form.

### Step 1 — Project overview
- `Project name` — required, **60 character limit**
- `Elevator pitch` — required, **200 character limit**
- `Thumbnail` — JPG / PNG / GIF, 5 MB max, 3:2 ratio recommended

### Step 2 — Project details
- `* About the project`
- `* Built with`
- `"Try it out" links`
- `Image gallery`
- `Choose files`
- `Video demo link`

Field notes:
- `About the project` — Markdown with LaTeX support. Devpost's own placeholder prompts for **Inspiration / What it does / How we built it / Challenges / Accomplishments / What we learned / What's next**.
- `Built with` — tag list, **max 25 tags** (languages, frameworks, platforms, cloud services, databases, APIs).
- `Try it out links` — repeatable URL field for demo site, app store listing, or GitHub repo.
- `Image gallery` — JPG / PNG / GIF, 5 MB max each, **up to 15 images**, 3:2 ratio recommended.
- `Video demo link` — must be a **YouTube, Facebook Video, Vimeo or Youku** URL. Embedded at the top of the project page.

## What to build / Requirements

1. Project Name
What's your project called? Keep it simple and memorable.

2. Tell Us About It
Walk us through your project in your own words:

- What problem are you solving, or what does your project do?

- How does it work?

- What are the main features?

- What tools, languages, or APIs did you use?

- Who is it built for?

3. Show Your Work (Required)
Upload at least one screenshot, video, or file that shows your project in action. We need to see it working — or at least what it looks like.

4. Project Link (Optional but Encouraged)
Got a GitHub repo or a live demo? Throw the link in. It helps judges get a better look.

 

5. Your Team
List everyone who worked on the project and what they contributed. Flying solo? Just put your name down.
